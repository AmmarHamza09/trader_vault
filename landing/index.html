Questions, answered

Is TraderVault a signal service?

No. It's a journal and coaching tool. It never gives buy/sell signals or price predictions.

Which platforms can I import from?

MetaTrader 5 (screenshot or the TraderVault EA) and TradingView (screenshots and webhook alerts, which need a TradingView plan that supports them). You can also log trades by hand.

Does it work for prop-firm challenges?

Yes — phases, profit targets, daily loss, and static or trailing max-loss, including instant funded accounts.

Who can see my trades?

Only you, unless you create a read-only share link for a single account. You can switch the link off any time.

Is the AI always right?

No. It can make mistakes, so imports are shown for your review before anything is saved, and coaching is for process and risk — not financial advice.
